How the Preflight Engine works

Four deterministic steps ensure complete economic transparency before you trade.

01

Select Asset & Spend

Pick your target tokenized stock (AAPLx, NVDAx, SPYx, TSLAx) and specify your intended payment in USDC or SOL.

02

Discover Live Route

JustFair queries Jupiter Swap V2 for the most optimal liquidity route across Raydium CLMM and Orca Whirlpools.

03

Apply Token Multipliers

The engine queries Token-2022 account parameters to calculate the true underlying stock share quantity delivered.

04

Measure Economic Truth

The delivered share value is priced against independent equity data to produce the final plain-money verdict.
