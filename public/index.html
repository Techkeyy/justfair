How JustFair Works

Five deterministic steps ensure complete economic transparency before you trade.

01

Choose a tokenized stock

Pick your target stock (e.g. AAPLx, NVDAx, SPYx, TSLAx) and enter what you plan to spend in USDC or SOL.

02

Check the Solana route

JustFair checks the available Solana route across supported liquidity to discover the best execution path.

03

Calculate stock exposure

The engine reads on-chain Token-2022 metadata and share multipliers to calculate the real stock exposure behind the tokens.

04

Compare with equity reference

The delivered exposure is compared against an eligible underlying-equity reference price.

05

Inspect the difference

You see the exact dollar difference between what you spend and what you receive before any money moves.

Truthful Market Stance

If the underlying stock market is closed or the reference cannot be verified, JustFair says so instead of guessing.
