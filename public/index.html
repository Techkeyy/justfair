How JustFair Works

A deterministic 4-step sequence ensures complete product truth and economic transparency.

01

Choose a company

Select from 12 canonical stocks (AAPL, NVDA, SPY, TSLA, etc.) to discover all verified representations on Solana.

02

State what matters to you

Specify your must-haves and preferences for self-custody, dividends, voting, redemption, and 24/7 trading.

03

Compare verified products

Product Preflight inspects on-chain Token-2022 mints and legal terms across issuers (xStocks, Ondo) to show matching products.

04

Check the trade fill

Execution Preflight verifies live DEX route economics against independent equity benchmarks before any money moves.

Truthful Market Stance

If the underlying stock market is closed or the reference cannot be verified, JustFair says so instead of guessing.

Developer & Wallet API

Built for users. Embeddable by wallets.

The Product Preflight and Execution Preflight engines can be integrated into wallets, trading interfaces, and tokenized-stock apps.

GET /api/v1/stocks
POST /api/v1/product-preflight 
curl -X POST https://justfair-theta.vercel.app/api/v1/product-preflight \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "underlying": "AAPL",
    "expectations": [
      { "capability": "HOLD_IN_OWN_WALLET", "priority": "MUST_HAVE" },
      { "capability": "ECONOMIC_DIVIDEND_BENEFIT", "priority": "MUST_HAVE" }
    ]
  }'
