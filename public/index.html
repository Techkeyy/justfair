JUSTFAIR · DEVELOPER ONBOARDING

Crash-test your stock app before users find the bugs.

Your proprietary code stays local. JustFair evaluates financial correctness against adverse market scenarios and real market infrastructure, with zero custody and zero telemetry.

STEP 1

Set up JustFair in your project

Initialize configuration and a clean protocol adapter scaffold in your project without cloning JustFair. No repo clone required, no account required, no funds, and no private keys.

npx justfair@latest init

Creates justfair.config.js and justfair-adapter.mjs in your current directory. Existing files are never overwritten.

STEP 2

Connect your app (The Observation Bridge)

JustFair needs a small observation adapter that reads values your real application calculates or displays.

Open justfair-adapter.mjs and point each observations assignment inside the POST /justfair/v1/evaluate handler at the value your app actually calculates or displays.

YOUR STOCK APP → OBSERVATION ADAPTER → JUSTFAIR → PASS / FAIL / UNABLE
EDIT justfair-adapter.mjs → POST /justfair/v1/evaluate 
if (scenarioId === "STALE_CARRIED_FORWARD_EQUITY") {
  // Replace with the price your app displays
  observations.displayedPrice =
    inputs?.referencePrice ?? 329.29;

  // Replace with whether your app claims it is live
  observations.claimsLive = true;
}
  • The adapter is the bridge: It translates your internal data into raw observations.
  • The adapter is NOT the product being tested: Your real application supplies the values.
  • Two separate ports: Your app can run on any localhost port. The adapter reads from it and exposes JustFair's two test endpoints on its own localhost port.
  • Observations only: Your app supplies what it displays; JustFair owns the financial judgment.
STEP 3

Start your app & adapter

Run your application (e.g. on port 4000) and start the observation adapter on localhost (e.g. port 3100).

node justfair-adapter.mjs

Adapter exposes GET /justfair/v1/manifest and POST /justfair/v1/evaluate on localhost (e.g. http://localhost:3100).

STEP 4

Run the financial crash test

Execute scenarios against your running adapter. Compatible scenarios run, incompatible scenarios skip honestly, invariants are evaluated with zero funds and zero signing, and Replay Lab launches locally at 127.0.0.1.

npx justfair@latest test --target http://localhost:3100 --open

Tests stale/carry-forward equity prices, PreStocks conversion expiries, Meteora DBC price impact, and Tessera Token-2022 transfer fees.

STEP 5

Fix → Rerun → Pass

When an invariant fails, see the exact breakdown: FAIL → WHAT HAPPENED → EXPECTED VS YOUR APP → ROOT CAUSE → FIX GUIDANCE → REPLAY. Fix your stock app, run the exact same command, and watch it turn green.

Upload your justfair-result.json artifact manually or explore interactive sample failures.
View adapter specification & protocol contract

GET /justfair/v1/manifest returns {"adapterVersion": "1", "name": "...", "capabilities": [...]}.

POST /justfair/v1/evaluate receives {"scenarioId", "scenarioVersion", "inputs"} and returns only observations — never a verdict. JustFair owns PASS / FAIL.

Localhost targets only. Responses capped at 64 KB. Safe for confidential protocols and air-gapped test environments.
